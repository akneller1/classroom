How to give it. Give one session per class period (about 60 minutes each, untimed for students who need it). The item types follow the Grade 7 MCAS: multiple choice (one answer), select all that apply (every correct choice and no wrong ones), short answer (a number or expression) and a four-part constructed response. Students may use the Grade 7 reference sheet at the end of this booklet in both sessions. This is a practice test written for this site, not a released MCAS test.

Grade 7 Mathematics Reference Sheet

Area

RectangleA = lw
ParallelogramA = bh
TriangleA = 12bh
TrapezoidA = 12(b1 + b2)h
CircleA = πr2

Circles

CircumferenceC = πd = 2πr
Diameterd = 2r
ππ ≈ 3.14 or π ≈ 227

Volume and surface area

Right prismV = Bh (B = area of the base)
Rectangular prismV = lwh
Surface areaSA = the sum of the areas of all the faces
CubeV = s3, SA = 6s2

Angles

Complementarymeasures add to 90°
Supplementarymeasures add to 180°
Vertical anglesequal measures
Triangleangle measures add to 180°

Percents and money

Percentpartwhole = percent100
Percent of changeamount of changeoriginal amount × 100%
Percent error|estimate − actual|actual × 100%
Simple interestI = Prt (r as a decimal, t in years)

Proportional relationships

Equationy = kx, where k = yx is the constant of proportionality
Unit ratethe value of y when x = 1

Probability

Theoretical probabilityP(event) = number of favorable outcomesnumber of possible outcomes
ComplementP(not A) = 1 − P(A)
Independent eventsP(A and B) = P(A) · P(B)

Conversions

Length1 ft = 12 in. · 1 yd = 3 ft · 1 mi = 5,280 ft
1 m = 100 cm · 1 km = 1,000 m · 1 in. = 2.54 cm
Capacity1 c = 8 fl oz · 1 pt = 2 c · 1 qt = 2 pt · 1 gal = 4 qt
1 L = 1,000 mL
Weight and mass1 lb = 16 oz · 1 T = 2,000 lb
1 kg = 1,000 g
Time1 h = 60 min · 1 yr = 52 weeks = 365 days
