How to play
  • Each fence is an inequality in the form y ? mx + b. Type the slope and y-intercept as fractions; put 1 on the bottom for whole numbers.
  • > or ≥ makes the safe zone above the line. < or ≤ makes it below.
  • With ≤ or ≥ the line is solid, so anyone standing on it is inside. With < or > the line is dashed, so anyone on it is outside.
  • On two-fence nights, the safe zone is where both inequalities are true at once.
  • Build it right on the first try for 3 stars, second try for 2, any try after that for 1.
