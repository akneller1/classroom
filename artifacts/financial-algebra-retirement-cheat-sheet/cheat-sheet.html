5Spending target: a scenario

Savings target=annual spending gapw\text{Savings target}=\dfrac{\text{annual spending gap}}{w}Savings target=wannual spending gap​
Gap = spending minus other reliable income; w = assumed starting withdrawal fraction.
$40,000 spending − $16,000 other income = $24,000 gap.
At assumed 4%: 24,0000.04=600,000\tfrac{24{,}000}{0.04}=600{,}0000.0424,000​=600,000.
A 4% scenario is not a guarantee. Include taxes, time horizon, fees and market risk; use consistent year dollars.

6Reminders & mistakes

✔ Do remember
  • Consider the full match when cash flow and plan terms allow.
  • Review saving, inflation and return assumptions over time.
✘ Common mistakes
  • Future balance = current wealth: convert purchasing power.
  • Match is always yours immediately: check vesting.
  • Social Security covers every cost: estimate your own gap.
  • A fixed return is certain: test lower-return scenarios.
