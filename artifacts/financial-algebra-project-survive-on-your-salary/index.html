Financial Algebra · Unit 2: Budgeting & Spending · Project

Survive on Your Salary

Draw a career, build a realistic monthly budget, and survive what life throws at you.

3–4 class periodsIndividual (best) or pairsStandard level

Your first full-time paycheck is coming. Before the excitement wears off, rent, a car, groceries, and a dozen small bills will compete for every dollar. In this project you will receive a fictional career, figure out what you actually take home, build a budget for life in the fictional city of Riverton, and then respond to the surprises that real life brings.
