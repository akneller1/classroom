Post-it Math Games

49 sorting, matching and building games for Grades 1–8, Algebra 1, Geometry and Algebra 2. Every game has Practice, Timed and Challenge modes, four levels from Easy to Expert, teacher customization and round codes.

How every game works

  • Practice has hints and explains every mistake. Timed saves best times. Challenge has 3 lives, a countdown and streak bonuses.
  • Levels: Easy and Medium build the skill; Hard adds the classic traps; Expert is built to stretch your strongest students.
  • Customize picks the skills, card style and round length. A round code gives everyone the same round, and Copy link shares your exact settings.
  • Options (saved for every game): text size, read cards aloud, plain font, high contrast, reduced motion (falling-card games become still boards), light or dark theme.
  • Everything works with drag-and-drop, tap-then-tap, or the keyboard (Tab, Enter, number keys). Scores are saved on the device; nothing is sent anywhere.
