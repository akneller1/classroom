4Reconcile the account

Subtract outstanding payments; add deposits in transit to the bank statement balance.
Example: Bank $1,250; uncleared check $80; deposit in transit $200.
Adjusted bank: 1,250−80+200=1,3701{,}250-80+200=1{,}3701,250−80+200=1,370
Your record: $1,385 minus unrecorded $15 bank fee
Adjusted record: 1,385−15=1,3701{,}385-15=1{,}3701,385−15=1,370
Answer: Both records agree at $1,370

5Match product to purpose

  • Daily bills: accessible checking with manageable fees.
  • Emergency fund: liquid savings with dependable access.
  • Known future date: compare a CD term with that date.
  • Check insurance and ownership limits directly.
  • Use alerts, protect login details and review statements; report unexpected transactions promptly.

6Reminders & mistakes

✔ Do remember
  • Convert months to years in simple interest.
  • Read the early-withdrawal terms before choosing a CD.
✘ Common mistakes
  • Highest APY always wins: include fees and access.
  • Deposit insurance covers stocks: it does not insure market losses.
  • Statement = spendable cash: pending items can change it.
  • APR = APY: compounding may make them differ.
