2Compare beyond the fee

The fee comparison favors A if risk, holdings and services are otherwise equal. Ask what each fund owns and whether advice or restrictions differ.
Expense ratio: annual fund cost as a percent of assets.
0.2% of $10,000 = $20 for one year at a constant balance.
Small recurring costs can compound into a large difference. Compare like with like.

6Reminders & mistakes

✔ Do remember
  • State return, deposit timing, fees and time period.
  • Keep money for near-term essential needs accessible.
✘ Common mistakes
  • Average return = guaranteed: real results fluctuate.
  • Diversification eliminates risk: it cannot prevent all losses.
  • Future dollars = today’s value: adjust for inflation.
  • Higher past return proves better: compare risk and costs too.
