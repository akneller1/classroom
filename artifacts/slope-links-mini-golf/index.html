    How to play
    • Every shot starts at the ball and follows a straight line with the slope you enter. You don't choose a y-intercept, because the line always passes through the ball.
    • Pick left or right, then a club. A club's number is how far the ball moves across (change in x). The ball rises or falls by the slope times that distance.
    • You won't see the path until you hit. Use rise over run between the ball and the cup to plan your slope.
    • The ball drops in if it reaches the cup with no more than 1 unit left to roll. Hit it too hard and it rolls right over.
    • Walls and sand stop the ball. Water costs a penalty stroke and puts the ball back.
    • After every shot, the ball settles on the nearest grid point. Adjust the slope and hit again.
