Balloon Festival

Compare four hot air balloons shown in four different ways.

2–3 class periodsCP2 · CP1 · HonorsScience & Flight

At the Sky Valley Balloon Festival, each balloon's altitude is given a different way: a table, an equation, a graph, and words. Students find rates of change and starting altitudes, write equations, predict when balloons meet or land, and design a balloon that meets festival rules.
