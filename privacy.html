Privacy

The short version: no accounts, no cookies, and nothing about students is collected. The site counts visits anonymously so it can tell which resources teachers use.

What the site never collects

  • Names, email addresses or logins. There are no accounts for teachers or students.
  • Student answers, scores or work. Games, readiness checks and Interactive Math run entirely in the browser, and nothing is submitted.
  • Anything that identifies a person, and there is no advertising or ad tracking.

Anonymous visit counts

The site uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service that uses no cookies. It records:

  • which pages are viewed, and roughly how long they stay open;
  • the referring site, country, device type and browser;
  • which resource cards and generator buttons (Build, Print, Download) are used.

Only the site owner sees these totals, and they are used only to decide what to improve or build next. They are not sold or shared.

To turn counting off in your browser, open the site once with ?analytics=off at the end of the address, for example https://mrksclassroom.com/?analytics=off. Use ?analytics=on to turn it back on.

What stays on your computer

Saved activities, lesson-plan edits and saved assessments are stored in your own browser. They are never sent to the site. Clearing your browsing data removes them; Back Up Saved Work downloads a copy you can keep.

Other services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, these services receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when a page loads. Links that leave the site, such as Buy Me a Coffee, follow that site's own privacy policy.
