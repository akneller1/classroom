About Mr. K's Classroom

Free, practical math teaching tools, built by a math teacher.

I'm Andrey Kneller, a high school math teacher at Keefe Regional Technical High School in Framingham, Massachusetts. I built Mr. K's Classroom to make it easier to prepare engaging math lessons without requiring students to create accounts.

The site brings together printable worksheets, assessments, lesson planning tools, classroom games, flashcards, and interactive explorations. My goal is to make these resources easy to adapt for real classrooms, including school Chromebooks.

Resources are free to use. Some teacher work is saved locally in your browser, so it may not follow you to another device. Check the teacher guide for details.
